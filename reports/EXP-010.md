## Gold-zone review

The current public-code ranking was inspected rather than trusting notebook
titles. The first positions were dominated by byte-identical Pipe16 productive
opening artifacts. The distinct Farmer John V55 branch retains that production
stack and adds two defensive mechanisms: restore the scheduled pasture if the
temporary wheat failed, and move executable cash-product sales ahead of final
zero-execution sale requests without changing fixed-price order positions.

Notebook payloads were extracted statically and hash-verified. Arbitrary
notebook code was not executed.
